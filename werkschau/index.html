The system

Not an app. An ecosystem around one phone.

Everything orbits the home screen: the tools you open most, the ones that look after you, and the bridge to the laptop.

01 — ARC·DNA HOME

A home screen you steer blind.

Hold a bottom corner and a fan of categories blooms along your thumb's arc. Slide out and the apps of that category open up. Let go to launch.

  • Built for one-handed use on a big phone
  • A lock keeps the category steady while you reach for the app
  • Ambient weather, battery and storage, drawn faint behind the clock
Live · hold the corner ◳ and slide
02 — STATISTIK-HOME

A launcher that knows the hour.

No icon wall. It ranks your apps from how often you use them, how recently, and whether this hour matches their pattern. Your real usage never leaves the phone.

  • Three tunable weights: frequency, recency, time of day
  • No internet permission at all
  • Swipe up for all apps, down to search, left for the statistics

03 — ARC LINK

The laptop becomes the tablet's keyboard.

Plug the tablet in and every key on the MacBook goes to Android. Unplug, and the Mac has it back within two seconds. The tablet even shows the laptop's battery.

  • Word delete, shortcuts, F-keys mapped to Back, Home, Recents
  • F9 quits, Ctrl always reaches the Mac
  • Free and open on this site
Live · typing over USB

04 — TAKT

A day planned around your body.

Fixed anchors stay put: waking, school, sleep. Everything else flows into the gaps, in the order your energy allows. Fall behind, and it re-plans instead of showing you a list of failures.

  • Heavy work in the freshest window, light tasks when energy dips
  • A warm room moves the hard things earlier
  • Runs offline, inside the launcher
Live · fall behind on purpose
05 — BÄR

Two pillars, not a hundred alarms.

Lights-out and wake-up are fixed. The hour of reading before sleep is built in. The bear holds the line when you want to renegotiate at midnight.

  • Backward-planned evening: read, lights out, wake
  • Warm dark palette with almost no blue light
  • Streak of nights on time
Live · your clock right now
How it's built

Three rules behind every screen.

IThe whole screen is the control.

No tiny targets. Swipes start anywhere, thumbs land in corners, and the most-used apps never move.

IINo server. Your phone.

Usage, plans and sleep stay on the device. No accounts, no ads, no trackers.

IIISteal from what works.

Learned from iOS, Pixel, One UI, HyperOS and ChromeOS. Then made quieter.
