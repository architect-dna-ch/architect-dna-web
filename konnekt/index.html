Concept & market study

Konnekt

An anti-loneliness platform where small real-world bubbles turn strangers into a lasting friend group — funded by cities and NGOs, not by selling attention.

The problem

Loneliness is now a measured public-health crisis, and it is worst among the young — the group that looks most "connected."

  • Switzerland: ~42% felt lonely sometimes or often (2022); roughly 1 in 5 feel lonely regularly, and the rate is higher under 36.
  • Young Europe: ~57% of 18–35-year-olds report moderate-to-strong loneliness (Bertelsmann); over half of young people in the EU feel lonely.
  • Existing social apps optimise for engagement (infinite scroll, ad impressions) — the opposite of the real-world contact that actually cures loneliness.

The insight

People already pay to meet strangers in person. Timeleft (dinner with strangers) connects ~150,000 diners a month across 200+ cities and is reportedly near $10M ARR on a ~$20/month subscription. Friendship apps drew ~$16M in US consumer spend and 4.3M downloads in 2025. The demand is proven — but one-off dinners don't fix loneliness. Repetition does.

Konnekt's wedge

  • Bubbles, not feeds. A bubble is a tiny local plan (2–8 people). Hosting takes 20 seconds; joining is one tap.
  • The group loop. Meet the same people across a few bubbles and Konnekt quietly forms a friend group — the retention engine competitors lack.
  • Loneliness-aware, not addiction-aware. A nightly nudge offers a plan, then gets out of the way. Success = you log off and go outside.
  • Non-extractive revenue. No ads, no data sale. Cities and NGOs pay for loneliness prevention; local venues pay for the foot traffic.

Inside ARC·DNA

Konnekt isn't a separate app you forget to open — it lives on the ARC·DNA home screen. The launcher already knows your free evenings (Takt), your mood (Mood), and when you've been alone too long. On a quiet night it can surface "a bubble starts in 30 min, 400 m away" right where you already look. ARC·Tap can even add someone you just met to your group with a phone-to-phone touch.

MVP → platform

  • Now (this prototype): host, discover and join live bubbles; auto-forming friend group — all real and shared across everyone who opens it.
  • Next: map + proximity, bubble chat, city verification for a safe senior programme, venue coupons.
  • Platform: PWA → Capacitor native app, PostgreSQL, FCM push, and the ARC·DNA home integration.
